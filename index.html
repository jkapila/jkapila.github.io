About

I founded Kriyalytics, an AI consulting and implementation company for manufacturing, industrial, mining, logistics, and oil and gas. My current focus is physical AI and IoT enablement: getting models to work on real machines and real sensor data.

Before this I worked as an AI solution architect and as an embedded AI CTO for client companies. Across those roles I kept seeing the same gap: building the model is the easy part, and explaining it to the business is where projects stall.

That gap is why I still write open-source code. mltreelib is my attempt to close it: models that handle messy data as it is and produce results a business can read and trust. More research packages and runnable snippets from my writing will land here.

Essays are on jitinkapila.com.
